Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Net-Metering work in Egypt?
Net-metering allows you to connect your solar system to the national grid, effectively turning the grid into a virtual battery. When your panels produce more electricity than your facility consumes during peak sun hours, the excess is exported to the grid, generating financial credits. At night or during cloudy days, you pull electricity back from the grid. At the end of the billing cycle, you are only billed for the "net" energy consumed. If you generate a surplus, credits roll over to the next month, culminating in an annual settlement. Vertsolar seamlessly handles all regulatory approvals, grid-impact studies, and paperwork with the NREA (New and Renewable Energy Authority) and your local electricity distribution company to ensure full compliance.

Do panels need to be cleaned due to dust in the MENA region?
Yes, regular cleaning is crucial in the MENA region. Accumulation of dust, sand, and pollution—known as "soiling"—can reduce system efficiency by 5-15% (and sometimes more after seasonal Khamaseen winds) if left unmanaged. To combat this, Vertsolar provides comprehensive Operations & Maintenance (O&M) contracts tailored to your specific site. These include scheduled manual cleaning or fully automated, waterless robotic dry-cleaning solutions to conserve precious water resources. Furthermore, we exclusively source premium panels featuring advanced anti-reflective and anti-soiling glass coatings that actively repel dust and require less frequent maintenance compared to standard modules.

What is the lifespan and warranty of the system?
A commercial-grade solar installation is a long-term asset designed to last over 25 years. We utilize exclusively Tier-1 components to ensure maximum reliability. Our solar PV modules come with a standard 12-15 year product warranty against manufacturing defects, paired with an industry-leading 25-30 year linear performance warranty (guaranteeing the panels will still produce at least 80-84% of their original output at year 25). String inverters typically feature a 5-10 year warranty, which can be extended up to 20 years upon request. Additionally, our customized mounting structures carry a 10-year warranty against structural degradation, ensuring your system withstands harsh weather conditions.

What is the typical Return on Investment (ROI) for a solar system?
Thanks to Egypt's high solar irradiance and rising commercial electricity tariffs, the financial returns are highly attractive. Most commercial and industrial solar installations pay for themselves within 3 to 5 years. Once the system has recovered its initial cost, you will enjoy over two decades of virtually free electricity, significantly lowering your operational expenses and shielding your business from future energy price hikes. Vertsolar provides detailed financial modeling, including projected savings, payback periods, and Internal Rate of Return (IRR), before any commitment is made.

How much space is required for a commercial solar installation?
As a general rule of thumb, you need about 10 square meters of unshaded roof space for every 1 kilowatt-peak (kWp) of solar capacity installed. For example, a 100 kWp system would require approximately 1,000 square meters. During our initial site assessment, our engineering team uses advanced 3D modeling and shading analysis to evaluate your roof's orientation, structural integrity, and obstacle profiles, ensuring we design the most space-efficient and high-yielding system possible for your facility.
